COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT - SUMMARY

Property: Westside Centre, 123 Main Street, Suite 400
Tenant: Acme Corporation
Landlord: Westside Properties LLC
Lease Term: January 1, 2024 - December 31, 2028 (5 years)
Base Rent: $25,000/month

OPERATING EXPENSE PROVISIONS (Article 4)

Clause 4.1 - Tenant's Share: Tenant shall pay 15% of Building Operating Expenses as Additional Rent.

Clause 4.2 - Included Expenses: Operating Expenses include:
- Real estate taxes and assessments
- Insurance premiums (property, liability, casualty)
- Utilities for common areas (electricity, water, gas)
- Janitorial and cleaning services for common areas
- Security services for the building
- Landscaping and grounds maintenance
- HVAC maintenance and repair for common areas
- Elevator maintenance and inspection
- Property management fees (capped at 4% of gross collected rents)
- Common area capital improvements (amortized over useful life)

Clause 4.3 - Excluded Expenses: The following are EXCLUDED from Operating Expenses and shall NOT be charged to Tenant:
- Capital expenditures for structural repairs (roof, foundation, load-bearing walls)
- Landlord's legal fees unrelated to building operations
- Landlord's marketing and leasing commissions
- Depreciation on building or equipment
- Costs to cure Landlord's defaults under this or other leases
- Penalties, fines, or late fees incurred by Landlord
- Costs of remediating environmental contamination existing prior to Lease commencement
- Landlord's overhead and administrative salaries not directly related to building operations
- Costs for services not actually provided to the building
- Expenses for space leased to other tenants not benefiting Tenant

Clause 4.4 - Audit Rights: Tenant shall have the right to audit Landlord's operating expense records within 180 days of receiving the annual reconciliation statement.

Clause 4.5 - Gross-Up Provision: If building occupancy is below 80%, Operating Expenses shall be grossed up to 80% occupancy for calculation purposes.
